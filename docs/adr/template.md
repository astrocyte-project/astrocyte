# ADR Template

## Title

Brief, descriptive title for the architectural decision.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded

## Context

What is the issue that we're seeing that is motivating this decision or change? What is the business or technical context?

Include:
- Business goals or requirements
- Technical constraints
- Current pain points
- Future considerations

## Decision

What is the change that we're proposing and/or doing?

Include:
- Clear statement of the decision
- Implementation details if relevant
- Scope of the decision

## Consequences

What becomes easier or more difficult to do because of this change?

Include:
- Positive consequences (benefits)
- Negative consequences (costs, trade-offs)
- Risks and mitigations
- Impact on other systems or teams

## Alternatives Considered

What other options have we considered?

For each alternative, include:
- Description of the option
- Pros and cons
- Why it was not chosen

## Related Decisions

Are there any related decisions that inform this one?

Link to other ADRs or relevant documentation.

## Notes

Any additional notes, implementation details, or follow-up items.
